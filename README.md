# Smart City Bike Mobility & Station Analytics Report/Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI data analytics project providing comprehensive insights into urban bike-sharing networks, station capacities, operational statuses, and availability trends.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This dashboard serves as a centralized intelligence system for monitoring city-wide bike mobility, evaluating contract efficiencies, and tracking real-time asset availability across multiple networks.

---

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Stations:** 1K (Active monitored bike stations)
* **Total Available Bikes:** 20K (Current available inventory across networks)
* **Total Bike Stands:** 57K (Total rack capacity deployed)
* **Closed Stations Count:** 1K
* **Open Stations Count:** 1K

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations & Sections

### 1. Status Distribution & Contract Analysis
* **Status Distribution:** Breakdown of Open vs. Closed stations (50% / 50% ratio).
* **Contract Analysis by City / Network:** Evaluates performance metrics and average bike counts across individual municipal contracts.
* **Banking Availability:** Detailed visualization tracking station stock levels and availability patterns.

### 2. Trends & Geospatial Mapping
* **Last Update & Occupancy Rate Trend:** Historical trend lines tracking occupancy fluctuations over time.
* **Top Contracts by Bike Stands:** Ranking of top-performing contracts based on total infrastructure capacity.
* **Bike Station Map & Status Overview:** Geographical mapping displaying spatial distribution and status markers (Open/Closed) across regions.

### 3. Summary & Advanced Analytics
* **Contract Status & Bike Availability Summary:** Tabular matrix showing detailed breakdowns per contract ID.
* **Bike Stand vs. Available Bike Analysis:** Scatter analysis correlating total stands with available inventory.
* **Dynamic Filtering:** Interactive slicer panel enabling users to filter data dynamically by Contract ID.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Tools
* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema, Custom Calculated Columns, Relationships
* **DAX Measures:** Aggregations, Status Counts, Percentage Calculations
* **Design Elements:** Custom Navigation Shapes, Bookmarks, Selection Panes, and Drill-through layers.
